01 · Platform & Scope

Platform ownership and mandate

I own RSA (Rapid SAP Adoption), an enterprise agentic AI platform built on SAP Business Technology Platform and running in production at three large multinational clients. It turns a client's process and system landscape into structured knowledge, and has agents produce analysis, configuration decisions and test cases with full traceability.

Accountable for

Reference architecture, ingestion and integration layers, guardrails, output-quality evaluation and the cost-per-task model applied across delivery teams.

How the platform works
01 Ingest
Ingests and integrates client process and system landscape data
02 Graph
Builds a knowledge graph of the landscape
03 Ontology
Derives a formal ontology from the graph
04 Agents
Agents produce analysis, configuration decisions and test cases, fully traceable
What I own

Reference architecture

The platform's reference architecture and the ingestion and integration layers that connect it to each client's landscape.

Guardrails and traceability

Guardrails on agent behaviour, and full traceability for every analysis, configuration decision and test case the agents produce.

Output-quality evaluation

Evaluation of the quality of what the agents produce, owned as part of the platform.

Cost-per-task model

The cost model applied across delivery teams, so AI cost is measured per unit of work.
